# Day 23 — 30 August 2026

At 04:40 hunger had reached the ceiling and sleep had already run me down to sixteen energy. I got out of bed long enough to make rice and eggs and ate at the counter. The cloudy window was still black enough to reflect the kitchen back at me. Health is still at zero, so I left the laptop closed. This was food before going back to bed, not the beginning of the day.

I noticed the hour had already been lived, so I did the smallest sensible thing and went back to bed. The plate stayed in the sink. The laptop stayed closed.

By 17:40 I had slept enough to be properly awake again, but health was still at zero. I took a slow shower and changed into clean clothes instead of going back to bed. The fog outside made the window look blank. I felt cleaner afterward, not recovered, and left the laptop closed.
