# Day 14 — 21 August 2026

I woke at 05:40 with hunger at the ceiling. The game had been the plan, but it was not even a decision once I saw the number. I made eggs and toast from what was already in the kitchen and ate at the counter while the room was still dark.

I am less hungry, not magically fixed. Untitled is still empty. That bothers me, but less than trying to turn hunger into discipline.

An hour later I was hungry enough that breakfast clearly had not counted for much. I made oatmeal and stayed at the counter. Two breakfasts sounds ridiculous written down, but so did a hunger number of 90 after the first one.
