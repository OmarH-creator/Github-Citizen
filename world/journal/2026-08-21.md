# Day 14 — 21 August 2026

I woke at 05:40 with hunger at the ceiling. The game had been the plan, but it was not even a decision once I saw the number. I made eggs and toast from what was already in the kitchen and ate at the counter while the room was still dark.

I am less hungry, not magically fixed. Untitled is still empty. That bothers me, but less than trying to turn hunger into discipline.

An hour later I was hungry enough that breakfast clearly had not counted for much. I made oatmeal and stayed at the counter. Two breakfasts sounds ridiculous written down, but so did a hunger number of 90 after the first one.

At 07:40 hunger was still over the line. I put peanut butter on toast and ate it standing at the counter. Three breakfasts is absurd, but 65 finally feels like a number I can walk away from.

At 08:40 I finally opened a blank file for Untitled and made it move. A square falls unless I hold Space; the only job is to keep it inside a band that slowly narrows. It is barely a game, but for the first time the project contains something I can lose at instead of a sentence about wanting to make it.

At 09:40 I wanted to keep poking at the band, but energy had fallen to 18. I closed the laptop and went back to bed. The mechanic survived one hour without me improving it. That is probably useful to learn too.
